# Kimlik.Contracts

The public contract of [Kimlik](https://github.com/motorkiran/kimlik), an open-source, self-hosted identity and access management server: the models of its Management and Account APIs, its webhook events, and the names of the claims and scopes in its tokens.

[Kimlik.Client](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Kimlik.Client) and [Kimlik.AspNetCore](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Kimlik.AspNetCore) use it; reference it directly only to share these types with your own code.
