# Code of conduct

Kimlik follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, accept constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and the people using it. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on [@motorkiran](https://github.com/motorkiran)'s GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may ban people from the project, temporarily or for good.
